Content & Platform Use Policies
1. Content Usage Policy
All educational materials provided through the ZakerHub platform are intended exclusively for students enrolled in ZakerHub programs. These materials may include class recordings, practice exams, worksheets, presentations, lesson notes, and other learning resources.
All platform content is protected under intellectual property and copyright laws and remains the property of ZakerHub or its authorized instructors.
Students are granted limited, non-transferable access to this content strictly for personal educational purposes related to their exam preparation.
Students may use the content for:
• Personal study and revision
• Reviewing recorded classes
• Completing assignments and practice exercises
• Improving understanding of course materials
However, users are strictly prohibited from engaging in the following activities:
• Uploading ZakerHub materials to other websites or platforms
• Sharing class recordings publicly or with individuals not enrolled in the program
• Selling, distributing, or redistributing course materials
• Copying or reproducing large portions of platform content for commercial use
• Using ZakerHub materials to create competing educational products
Unauthorized use, sharing, or distribution of platform content may result in:
• Immediate suspension or termination of the user account
• Removal of access to platform services
• Legal action where applicable under intellectual property laws
ZakerHub actively protects its educational materials to maintain the quality and value of its learning programs.
________________________________________
2. Account Termination Policy
ZakerHub reserves the right to suspend, restrict, or permanently terminate user accounts that violate platform rules, policies, or terms of service.
Account termination decisions are made to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for all students and instructors.
Accounts may be suspended or terminated for reasons including, but not limited to:
• Harassment, bullying, or inappropriate behavior toward instructors or other students
• Disruptive behavior during live classes or in communication channels
• Sharing account access with other individuals
• Unauthorized distribution or sharing of platform content
• Violating academic integrity policies
• Attempting to bypass platform rules or technical systems
• Any activity that harms the platform, its instructors, or its students
In some cases, ZakerHub may issue warnings or temporary suspensions before terminating an account. However, serious violations may result in immediate account termination without prior notice.
If an account is terminated due to policy violations, the user may lose access to:
• Live classes
• Recorded lessons
• Platform resources and materials
• Communication channels associated with the platform
Account termination decisions are made at the discretion of ZakerHub to ensure the integrity and safety of the educational environment.
________________________________________
3. Complaint & Dispute Resolution Policy
ZakerHub aims to maintain high standards of service and encourages students to communicate any concerns or issues they may experience while using the platform.
Students who encounter problems related to classes, instructors, scheduling, technical issues, or platform services may submit a formal complaint or support request.
Complaints may be submitted through the ZakerHub support team using the following communication channels:
• Official ZakerHub email address
• WhatsApp support number listed on the website
• The platform support system (when available)
When submitting a complaint, students are encouraged to provide clear details including:
• The nature of the issue or concern
• The date and time of the class or event involved
• The instructor or class related to the issue
• Any relevant supporting information
The ZakerHub support team will review submitted complaints and may take appropriate actions, including:
• Investigating the reported issue
• Communicating with the instructor or relevant parties
• Providing clarification or support to the student
• Implementing corrective measures when necessary
ZakerHub aims to review and respond to complaints within a reasonable time frame.
While the platform will make reasonable efforts to resolve disputes fairly, ZakerHub reserves the right to make final decisions regarding platform policies, class management, and operational procedures.

________________________________________

Support requests and complaints may be submitted through the following official ZakerHub communication channels:
• Email: zakerhub@gmail.com
• WhatsApp: The official support number listed on the ZakerHub website
The ZakerHub support team will review all submitted inquiries and complaints carefully and respond within a reasonable timeframe.
ZakerHub is committed to addressing concerns in a fair, transparent, and professional manner, while ensuring that all resolutions remain consistent with the platform’s policies and operational standards.
